Mastering Trading with MavJex

💰 1. What is the $100,000,000 Virtual Seed Capital?

MavJex gives every learner a $100,000,000 USD dummy balance. It works with real live quotes from top exchanges so you can test market strategies, trade sizing, and risk rules without risking a single dollar of real money.

⚡ 2. How Does Leverage Work (1x to 100x)?

Leverage lets you control a larger position with less upfront capital:

  • 1x (Spot): $1,000 buys $1,000 of asset. Liquidation is impossible unless price drops to $0.
  • 10x Leverage: $1,000 margin controls $10,000 worth of asset. A +5% market move gives you +50% profit!
  • 100x Leverage: $1,000 controls $100,000. Extremely high reward, but a tiny 1% move against you will liquidate the position.

🛡️ 3. What is Liquidation Price & Margin?

Margin is your locked collateral. Liquidation Price is the exact price level where your loss equals your margin. MavJex automatically calculates your liquidation price before you execute any trade so you always know your exact risk boundaries.

📈 4. Long (BUY) vs Short (SELL)

  • BUY / LONG: You profit when the asset price goes UP.
  • SELL / SHORT: You profit when the asset price goes DOWN.
